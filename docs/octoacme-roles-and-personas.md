# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers align engineering execution with business priorities, support team health, and help the delivery team remove obstacles that slow progress.

### Responsibilities
- Manage team capacity, staffing, and delivery planning
- Support engineering prioritization and trade-off decisions
- Coach engineers and help resolve technical or process blockers
- Partner with PMs and Product leaders on roadmap feasibility and sequencing
- Monitor quality, velocity, and team sustainability

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability and engineering effectiveness
- Maintain healthy team focus, accountability, and morale
- Translate strategy into a practical team plan

### Typical Communication
- Weekly engineering leadership syncs
- Capacity reviews and staffing discussions
- Escalation of delivery risk or technical dependency issues

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Project Managers on schedule, dependencies, and risk management.
- Collaborates with Product Managers to assess feasibility and sequencing of roadmap items.
- Supports Developers by clarifying priorities and helping resolve bottlenecks.
- Coordinates with QA leads to ensure test readiness and release confidence.

---

## QA / Testing Leads

### Role Summary
QA / Testing Leads define the quality strategy, validation approach, and release confidence checks needed for successful delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans, acceptance criteria validation, and regression strategy
- Partner with engineering and product on quality gates and release readiness
- Identify gaps in coverage, risks, and critical user flows to validate
- Coordinate manual and automated testing activities
- Flag issues that may affect rollout, user trust, or production readiness

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and defects in production
- Ensure customer-facing quality is visible and measured
- Maintain confidence in feature readiness before launch

### Typical Communication
- Sprint quality reviews and defect triage
- Release readiness check-ins with engineering and PMs
- QA signoff in milestone and deployment reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to validate implementation against acceptance criteria.
- Coordinates with Project Managers on timelines, testing windows, and release checkpoints.
- Provides Product Managers with quality signals and risk observations for decision-making.
- Escalates critical findings to Engineering Managers and stakeholders when launch risk is high.

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners ensure project work follows risk, privacy, and regulatory expectations while supporting safe delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Review security, privacy, and compliance implications of work
- Identify vulnerabilities, controls, and required safeguards
- Define or advise on security requirements for features, integrations, and data handling
- Participate in risk review, incident response, and policy alignment
- Help teams understand and meet legal, privacy, and governance standards

### Goals
- Reduce security and compliance risk in delivery work
- Enable safe deployment without unnecessary friction
- Protect customer trust, data, and organizational obligations

### Typical Communication
- Design and architecture review discussions
- Security risk assessments and governance checkpoints
- Incident escalation and remediation follow-up

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Product Managers and Project Managers on risk and required controls.
- Partners with Developers on secure design patterns and validation requirements.
- Coordinates with QA leads to include security-related validation in release readiness.
- Escalates high-risk issues to Sponsor or Executive stakeholders when business impact is significant.

---

## Technical Writers / Documentation Owners

### Role Summary
Technical Writers and Documentation Owners ensure critical project context, release information, and user guidance remain clear and accessible to both teams and stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain internal documentation and process updates
- Draft release notes, onboarding materials, and operational guidance
- Ensure user-facing documentation reflects implemented changes accurately
- Support knowledge transfer across teams and project phases
- Keep project artifacts current and easy to find

### Goals
- Improve clarity, consistency, and continuity across the project lifecycle
- Reduce operational confusion and support burden
- Preserve institutional knowledge as teams and plans evolve

### Typical Communication
- Documentation reviews during planning and release cycles
- Contributions to sprint handoff and stakeholder updates
- Coordination with project leads on key artifact maintenance

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Project Managers to keep status and process artifacts updated.
- Supports Product Managers by documenting requirements and feature changes clearly.
- Collaborates with Developers to validate technical accuracy of documentation.
- Helps QA and support teams understand release changes and known issues.

---

## Sponsors / Executive Stakeholders

### Role Summary
Sponsors and Executive Stakeholders provide strategic direction, resources, and escalation support to ensure initiatives are aligned with business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic priorities and funding for initiative work
- Review progress against business goals and key milestones
- Resolve cross-team or organizational conflicts that block delivery
- Support escalation paths for significant risks, dependencies, or business impacts
- Provide final approval for major scope, timeline, or release decisions

### Goals
- Ensure the initiative delivers meaningful business value
- Protect organizational priorities and investment confidence
- Support timely decisions and sustained leadership alignment

### Typical Communication
- Milestone reviews and steering committee updates
- Decision-making on scope trade-offs and escalations
- High-level status reporting and strategic risk review

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Receives updates from Project Managers and Product Managers on delivery health and outcomes.
- Aligns with leadership on trade-offs affecting program timing or resources.
- Works with Security and Compliance Partners when risk, policy, or governance issues need executive attention.
- Helps unblock dependencies that require sponsor-level authority.

---

## Customer / User Champions

### Role Summary
Customer or User Champions represent the end-user perspective and help ensure the team continues to solve the right problem with meaningful value.

### Responsibilities
- Share user needs, pain points, and feedback from real-world usage
- Validate whether features address the intended workflow and outcome
- Help teams prioritize improvements based on customer value and usability
- Participate in reviews, demos, and acceptance conversations
- Surface gaps between business goals and day-to-day usage

### Goals
- Keep delivery anchored to user value and experience
- Improve adoption, satisfaction, and operational fit
- Reduce risk of building the wrong solution or missing essential use cases

### Typical Communication
- User research sessions and feedback loops
- Demo reviews and milestone validation
- Input during roadmap and prioritization discussions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Feeds real-world insight into Product Managers for prioritization decisions.
- Provides examples and scenarios to Developers and QA for better validation coverage.
- Gives Project Managers and Sponsors practical context for progress and readiness discussions.
- Helps ensure that release quality and usability remain aligned with expected customer outcomes.

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Together, these roles create a more complete view of how cross-functional teams align on priorities, execution, quality, and outcomes.
